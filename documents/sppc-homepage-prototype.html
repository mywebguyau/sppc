Welcome home

You don't need to know what a "Holy Raza" is to walk through our doors.

Sts Peter & Paul has been gathering Cecil Park's Assyrian community — and increasingly, anyone curious about the ancient Church of the East — since our doors first opened. Every service is conducted in English, the words themselves carried over from the Aramaic that Christ and His apostles spoke.

We're a small parish with a big history: apostolic in origin, liturgical in worship, and genuinely warm in how we welcome the people who walk in not knowing what to expect.

"I have become all things to all people, so that by all possible means I might save some." — 1 Corinthians 9:22

Plan your visit

Three steps, no guesswork.

Like the cross of the Church of the East standing on three steps at Golgotha, visiting us is just three simple ones.

STEP ONE

Choose a service

Join us for Sunday morning Holy Raza (Divine Liturgy) at 9:00am, or come along to Wednesday Bible study or Friday evening preaching — all in English, all welcome.

STEP TWO

Find your way

We're at 32–40 Kosovich Place, Cecil Park, with parking on site. Arrive a few minutes early — someone will be at the door to greet you.

STEP THREE

Come as you are

No dress code, no expectations. Families and children are part of every service. Stay after for tea and a chance to meet people.

What we believe

Apostolic. Liturgical. Unmistakably ancient.

Read our full statement of faith →

An apostolic churchThe Holy Apostolic Catholic Assyrian Church of the East traces its founding to the apostles Thomas, Bartholomew, and Addai — among the oldest continuous Christian traditions in the world.

Worship rooted in scriptureOur liturgy, the Holy Raza, draws directly from the words and language of the early church, translated faithfully into English for our community today.

One faith, every generationWe hold to the historic Christian creeds while building a community where English-speaking families, young people, and newcomers all belong.
