## About — What We Believe

**Eyebrow:** Our faith
**H1:** Apostolic. Ancient. Alive today.

The Holy Apostolic Catholic Assyrian Church of the East is one of the oldest continuous Christian traditions in the world, tracing its founding to the apostolic mission of St Thomas, St Bartholomew, and St Addai in the first century.

**What sets us apart:**
- **Apostolic succession** — an unbroken line of bishops reaching back to the early church.
- **The Holy Raza** — our Divine Liturgy, rooted in ancient Syriac Christian worship, celebrated today in English for our congregation.
- **The cross without the corpus** — you'll notice our cross stands plain, on three steps representing Golgotha, rather than depicting the crucified Christ — a distinct visual and theological tradition within Eastern Christianity.
- **Historic creeds** — we hold to the faith handed down from the apostles and affirmed by the early church councils.

We are not the same as the Roman Catholic Church, the Eastern Orthodox Church, or other Eastern-rite churches — though we share deep historical roots with all of Christendom. If you're curious about exactly how, we're glad to talk about it after a service.
